How to play

The setup

Up to sixteen gamblers walk into a casino. One to four of them are killers. Nobody knows who.

Gamblers

Twelve games, one crew bank. Blackjack, roulette, slots, Crash, Plinko, Mines, Dragon Tower, the duck race, street dice, the prize wheel and the coin flip. Pick your bet; the max grows with the bank. Hit the target and the house is broken. Or vote every killer out.

The killer

Poison an unattended drink, lace the snack bowl, stab someone alone, or kill the lights. Do it when nobody is looking.

Watch your drink

You only see what you are looking at. Sit at a table and your drink goes on the rail behind you. Look back now and then. Sniff before you sip.

Thirst and snacks

Get thirsty and you slow down, so you have to drink. Pretzels make your wins pay more for a while, if they're clean.

Meetings

Find a body or ring the pit bell. Everyone says what they saw (the killer lies), then votes. Your notes remember who lurked by whose drink.

Keys: mouse to look, WASD to walk, E or click to use, F sip, R sniff, X pour, hold Space to look behind you, 1/2/3 killer moves, Esc menu. At a table you play with your hands: aim at a chip, a square on the felt or a button and click (or E) to press it, right-click a stack to take it back; walk to stand up. Phone: left thumb walks, right thumb drags to look, buttons on the right; at a table, tap the chips, squares and buttons.
