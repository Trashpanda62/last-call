How to play

The setup

Up to thirty-two gamblers walk into a casino. One to eight of them are killers. Nobody knows who.

Gamblers

Fourteen games, one crew bank. Blackjack, roulette, slots, Crash, Plinko, Mines, Dragon Tower, the duck race, street dice, the prize wheel the coin flip, skee-ball, coin-pusher and prize claw. Pick your bet; the max grows with the bank. Hit the target and the house is broken. Or vote every killer out.

The killer

Mix a poisoned drink at the bar and send it to anyone, rig an empty machine or the snack bowl, knife someone from behind, shove them into the duck pond or drop a chandelier on a table. Then wear your victim's face for a while. Do it when nobody is looking.

The blackout

A killer can cut the power (6), then press Q to turn into a giant mutated potato monster. It kills everyone it catches, as many as it can catch, until the crew resets the breakers by flashlight: left-click grabs whoever is in reach, Shift leaps a few metres at whoever is ahead (a miss leaves it winded). Sprint, hide, keep your flashlight off.

Stay alive

You only see what you are looking at, and knives come from behind. Free drinks might be poisoned, and anyone can poison one: sniff before you sip. The shady guy in the VIP alcove sells for your winnings, up to a revolver. Check a game before you play it. Poisoned? You have a few seconds to reach the antidote at the bar. A killing is heard all over its room, and only faintly by someone right against its walls.

Thirst and hunger

Get thirsty or hungry and you slow down, so drink at the bar and eat at the SNACKS stands (six around the casino). Snacks also make your wins pay more for a while, if they're clean.

Security and ghosts

Sit at the security desk and the cameras come on: watch any room from there. The crew can also hire a guard who reports what they see. The dead can haunt one person a round; the bots near them get a bad feeling.

Killer edges

Before the night starts each killer picks one edge: a witness meter, a camera over the shoulder, a sense that shows gamblers through walls, the STAFF ONLY doors, light feet, night eyes or a jackpot siren that pulls the crowd. Gamblers see the list too.

Meetings

Find a body or ring the pit bell. Everyone says what they saw (the killers lie), then votes. Your notes remember what you saw.

Keys: mouse to look, WASD to walk, left click to use, right click for action icons (sip, sniff, discard or check traps), G the mixer (at the bar), Q fire the revolver (J for killers), Shift sprint (5 seconds), Space marks whoever you aim at as a suspect (again: trusted), killer left-click murder, right-click action icons, Q transform in a blackout, Shift leap as the monster, E selected ability (hold to peek, or at a STAFF ONLY door for vents), U interact, 1-6 alternate killer moves, H haunt and C cameras (as a ghost), Esc menu. At a game you just walk up and stand there, free to look round and move: scroll the mouse wheel to pick your chip (it shows at the bottom), click a glowing spot on the felt to put chips there, right-click to take them back. Then click the game's own control to play: Hit deals at blackjack, pull a slot's lever, click the roulette or prize wheel, the dice, the coin, Plinko's green DROP button, or the first tile or egg. Blackjack’s Hit, Stand and Double controls sit directly below your betting circle (or press H, J and K). At the duck race place chips on a numbered duck circle and press START; at the coin-pusher or claw press START, position the chute or claw and press DROP; at Crash press Space to commit them for each rocket. Walk away to leave: a spin, a drop or a slot on AUTO keeps going without you, so you can set a row of slots spinning; a hand of blackjack, a Mines board, a Tower climb, a coin pot or a dice point settles once you are 10 ft away. The screen stays quiet: one message at a time at the top, thirst and hunger in the bottom-left corner (they turn red and grow when low), and action icons at the bottom center, dimmed while they recharge. On a phone the left thumb walks, the right drags to look, and the buttons by the right thumb are the ones you can use right now: Use always; Sniff, Sip, Discard and Check traps in the bottom action bar, Light in a blackout, Shoot with a loaded revolver, Moves for a killer's moves and Pounce for the monster.
