How to play

The setup

Up to thirty-two gamblers walk into a casino. One to eight of them are killers. Nobody knows who.

Gamblers

Twelve games, one crew bank. Blackjack, roulette, slots, Crash, Plinko, Mines, Dragon Tower, the duck race, street dice, the prize wheel and the coin flip. Pick your bet; the max grows with the bank. Hit the target and the house is broken. Or vote every killer out.

The killer

Mix a poisoned drink at the bar and send it to anyone, rig an empty machine or the snack bowl, knife someone from behind, shove them into the duck pond or drop a chandelier on a table. Then wear your victim's face for a while. Do it when nobody is looking.

The blackout

A killer can cut the power (6), then press T to turn into a giant mutated potato monster. It kills everyone it catches, as many as it can catch, until the crew resets the breakers by flashlight: 3 grabs whoever is in reach, E pounces a few metres at whoever is ahead (a miss leaves it winded). Sprint, hide, keep your flashlight off.

Stay alive

You only see what you are looking at, and knives come from behind. Free drinks might be poisoned, and anyone can poison one: sniff before you sip. The shady guy in the bar's corner sells for your winnings, up to a revolver. Check a game before you play it. Poisoned? You have a few seconds to reach the antidote at the bar. A killing is heard all over its room, and only faintly by someone right against its walls.

Thirst and hunger

Get thirsty or hungry and you slow down, so drink at the bar and eat at the SNACKS stands (six around the casino). Pretzels also make your wins pay more for a while, if they're clean.

Security and ghosts

Sit at the security desk and the cameras come on: watch any room from there. The crew can also hire a guard who reports what they see. The dead can haunt one person a round; the bots near them get a bad feeling.

Killer edges

Before the night starts each killer picks one edge: a witness meter, a camera over the shoulder, a sense that shows gamblers through walls, the STAFF ONLY doors, light feet, night eyes or a jackpot siren that pulls the crowd. Gamblers see the list too.

Meetings

Find a body or ring the pit bell. Everyone says what they saw (the killers lie), then votes. Your notes remember what you saw.

Keys: mouse to look, WASD to walk, E or click to use, F sip, R check (a drink, machine or snack bowl), X pour, G the mixer (at the bar), Q fire the revolver, Shift sprint (5 seconds), Space marks whoever you aim at as a suspect (again: trusted), 1-6 killer moves, T turn into the monster in a blackout (then 3 grab, E pounce), 7 and hold V for some killer edges, H haunt (as a ghost), Esc menu. At a game you just walk up and stand there, free to look round and move: scroll the mouse wheel to pick your chip (it shows at the bottom), click a glowing spot on the felt to put chips there, right-click to take them back. Roulette, blackjack, dice, the coin flip and the wheel then start by themselves after a short countdown (the game's big button starts one at once), and your chips ride each round until you take them back; at the duck race and Crash your chips are your bet for the next race or rocket. Walk away to leave; a round you leave is played out for you. The screen stays quiet: one message at a time at the top, thirst and hunger in the bottom-left corner (they turn red and grow when low), and a killer's moves at the bottom, folded to their key while they wait. On a phone the left thumb walks, the right drags to look, and the buttons by the right thumb are the ones you can use right now: Use always, Sip, Check and Pour with a drink in hand (Check at a game too), Light in a blackout, Shoot with a loaded revolver, Moves for a killer's moves and Pounce for the monster.
