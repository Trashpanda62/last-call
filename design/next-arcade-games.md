# Arcade games and remaining ideas

Potato Stack, Vault Breaker, Last Train, Midnight Pinball and Potato Pop are now implemented with generated art. See [controls, previews and verification](arcade-five/README.md). Gold Hook, Brick Breaker, Crossroad Heist, Orbit Escape and Rhythm Vault remain proposals.

| Game | Controls | Why add it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Potato Stack | Left click to drop a moving block | Overhang gets trimmed; increasingly narrow timing windows. Implemented. |
| Last Train | Left click to jump, right click to slide | Automatic runner through a haunted station, escalating obstacles. |
| Vault Breaker | Left click when rotating lock marks align | One-button precision; successive tumblers accelerate. |
| Gold Hook | Left click to fire a swinging hook | Catch valuables; heavy junk costs time. |
| Midnight Pinball | Left/right click operate corresponding flippers | Implemented with physical ball, bumpers, rails and two flippers; three balls. |
| Brick Breaker | Mouse moves paddle; left click launches | Familiar ricochet skill with escalating ball speed. |
| Crossroad Heist | Left click advances, right click retreats | Cross traffic and moving platforms while carrying stolen gold. |
| Orbit Escape | Left click to jump to the next rotating ring | Commit to transfers through changing gaps. |
| Rhythm Vault | Left/right click follow two beat lanes | Short rhythm challenges and streak bonuses. |
| Potato Pop | Left click targets, avoid innocent guests | Implemented: hit friendly heads and avoid red-eyed decoys. |

The remaining five ideas have not been added. They can use short rounds, one stake, host-owned outcomes and bounded payouts.
