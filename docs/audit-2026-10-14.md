## Audit: game logic and multiplayer

A read-only audit found 17 real bugs, 15 of them proved with scripts against the sim. All are fixed:
- A guest could freeze the host by sending a huge look angle: angles are checked and wrapped once.
- The host believed any table result a guest sent: a result now counts only if that table can pay it, at a game the
  player is at or left under 8 s ago, at the pace its rounds really run, with at most 5,000 waiting.
- A killer could empty the crew bank with losing bets in seconds: the pit boss cuts a killer off after losing 15% of
  the bank in a minute.
- Repeating an accusation in the meeting chat stacked without limit: one accusation and one vouch per speaker per
  meeting, 25 chat lines each, 200 a meeting.
- Bot killers' poisoned orders mostly failed (they stopped short of the bar): they use the same reach as the order.
- Built-in names like `__proto__` got through the room and edge lookups: only real entries count.
- Any PeerJS hiccup threw a guest out of the game: only losing the host does.
- A hidden host tab ran the game at quarter speed: its tick now carries up to 1.5 s of play.
- The antidote left the poisoned drink in your hand: it pours it out.
- A rigged game's fuse survived a meeting: meetings put burning fuses out (the game stays rigged).
- R with a drink in hand never checked a game: the game or snack bowl you face comes first.
- Checking a rigged game you had just taken said it was fine: it finds the rig and disarms it.
- Message floods made every snapshot huge: empty stakes are ignored and the chat is capped.
- The guests' speed limit ignored thirst, hunger and the peek: it counts them.
- The antidote prompt gave poisoning away at the first sip: it shows once the symptoms start.
- The crowd kept acting after the game was decided in the same tick: the tick stops there.
- "3 killer remains": plurals fixed.

## Graphics

Settings > Graphics: Low, Medium, High (default) and Ultra presets, or each option on its own:
- Resolution: render resolution 50 to 200%, filled to the screen by AMD FSR 1.0 (with sharpening) or bilinear; dynamic
  resolution that holds a target frame rate.
- Image: FXAA, SMAA, MSAA 4x or 8x; shadows up to 4096; GTAO ambient occlusion (half or full resolution); screen-space
  reflections on the polished floors, marble, chrome and gloss (Ultra turns them on); how many lamps light the room
  round you; texture filtering up to 16x.
- Light: bloom off, subtle or strong; the light cones over the tables; AgX, ACES or Neutral tone mapping, matched so the
  brightness slider means the same on each.
- Display: field of view, a frame rate limit, an FPS readout (with the internal and output resolution), fullscreen.

## Voice

- In-game proximity runs straight between the players' browsers over the game's own connection. Lobby, edge pick and
  results: everyone hears everyone. Play: you hear the living near you, full volume to 2.5 m and nothing past 14 m,
  panned left or right, quieter and muffled through walls. The dead hear each other anywhere; the living never hear
  them. Meetings: everyone hears the living.
- Browsers only give a page the microphone over https, so the preview is one link: https://10.42.0.37:3161/ (a
  self-signed certificate; each browser accepts it once). The old http link (:3160) now forwards there.
- Discord mode: the host's game tells the bot who is where; each player links once with `/lastcall link code:1234`
  (the code shows in the game). The bot moves only people already in a voice channel on the server, at most 8 moves
  every 10 s (Discord's rate limit), so sorting 10 players into a meeting takes about 10 s.
